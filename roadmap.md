# deli.africa roadmap

## v1.0 — sales-ready webshop

- Full-page checkout with basket summary
- Packeta parcel-point selection; no home delivery
- Barion hosted payment and verified callback
- Billingo automatic invoicing after successful payment
- Customer profile with saved contact details, preferred Packeta point and order history
- Admin order list, status workflow and product availability/promotion controls
- Approved Terms and Conditions and Privacy Notice linked during checkout

## v1.1 — retention and merchandising

- Curated collection and bundle landing pages
- Customer collections and loyalty points
- Admin upload and assignment for official logos, category montages, hero images and product photography
